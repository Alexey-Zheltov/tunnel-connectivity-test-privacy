Privacy Policy – Tunnel Connectivity Test

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Summary

Tunnel Connectivity Test (“the app”), published by Aleksey Zheltov (“the developer”), does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

How the app works

You enter a host name, IP address, port or URL and run DNS, TCP, TLS or HTTP checks. The requests are sent directly from your device to the hosts you specify. The results are shown on your screen only and are not sent to the developer or to any third party. The app does not store what you type or the results after it is closed.

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, which is needed to perform the checks you start.

Data sharing

The app does not share data with third parties. Servers that you choose to connect to may log your device’s IP address and request details in accordance with their own policies.

Data security, retention and deletion

The app does not collect or store personal or sensitive user data, so there is no such data to secure, retain, export or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your device.

Children

The app is a technical tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Developer: Aleksey Zheltov. Questions about this policy: alzhelt@gmail.com
